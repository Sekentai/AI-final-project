The recording shows the AI Fundamentals parking simulation and its three independent methods:

- **BFS:** shortest route, obstacles, and car movement.
- **Bayesian inference:** occupancy probability from a noisy sensor.
- **One-step MDP:** compare DRIVE with RECHECK.

It also illustrates the Mall, Campus and Stadium parking scenarios.
